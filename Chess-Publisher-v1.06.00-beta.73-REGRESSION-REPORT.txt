Verified Q214-Q216 behavior
- Default with no policy setting is FIDE_FIRST.
- First rating is the earliest stored official rating period, not current array order or current Tournament Rating.
- Ordinary players without rating history preserve existing Tournament Rating semantics.
- ROUND_EFFECTIVE passes the actual encounter round to beta.70 ratingForRound().
- ARO/TPR/PTP calculations consume round-effective opponent ratings under ROUND_EFFECTIVE.
- APRO/APPO derive from opponent performances computed under the same policy.
- RTNG/RTNG-R consume policy-derived own rating; ROUND_EFFECTIVE uses current standings round.
- USER_SELECTED requires valid selections for every multi-rating player while Article-10 tie-breaks are active.
- Missing/stale selections stop calculation before legacy derived state is changed.
- Policy changes do not change Official Rating, Tournament Rating, Starting Number, pairingNumber, player IDs, pairings or historical rating records.
- New metric caches are WeakMap-backed and tournament state remains JSON-serializable.
- tieBreakRatingPolicy is portable and is not filtered by the existing Cloud portable sanitizer.
