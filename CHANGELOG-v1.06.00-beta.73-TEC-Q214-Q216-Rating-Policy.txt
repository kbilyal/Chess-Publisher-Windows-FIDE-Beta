Implemented
- Q215: FIDE C.07 Article 10 first-rating policy is now the default for rating-based tie-breaks when multiple official ratings exist.
- Q214: explicit ROUND_EFFECTIVE policy resolves each opponent's rating for the actual encounter round through the existing beta.70 ratingForRound() foundation.
- Q216: explicit USER_SELECTED policy allows a validated official rating period to be selected per multi-rating player.
- Missing or stale USER_SELECTED choices block rating-based tie-break calculation before the legacy calculator mutates derived state; no silent fallback or guessing.
- Article-10 values covered by the additive layer: ARO and ARO cut/median forms, TPR, PTP, APRO, APPO, RTNG and RTNG/R.
- ROUND_EFFECTIVE RTNG uses the rating valid at the current standings round.
- Rating policy is portable tournament-rule state (`tournament.tieBreakRatingPolicy`) and is retained by the existing generic Cloud sanitizer.
- Tie-Break Settings exposes: FIDE default — First rating; Rating valid for each round; Explicit rating selection per player.
- Explicit-selection UI lists player identity, official rating, applicable round range and source period.
- Derived beta.73 metric caches are WeakMap-backed and do not add serializable player payload fields.
